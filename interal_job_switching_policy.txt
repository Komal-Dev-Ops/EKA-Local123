Internal Job Switching Policy
Document ID: HR-POL-INT-001 Company: Acme Technologies Pvt. Ltd. Version: 2.1 Effective Date: January 1, 2026 Policy Owner: Human Resources Review Frequency: Annual Status: Active

1. Purpose
The Internal Job Switching Policy establishes a transparent process through which eligible employees can apply for open positions within Acme Technologies.
The purpose of this policy is to:
	•	Encourage internal career growth and mobility.
	•	Help employees explore opportunities aligned with their skills and career goals.
	•	Retain organizational knowledge and experienced employees.
	•	Provide business teams with access to internal talent.
	•	Establish consistent eligibility, approval, and transfer procedures.
This policy applies to all permanent employees of Acme Technologies Pvt. Ltd., unless otherwise stated.

2. Scope
This policy applies to internal applications for:
	•	Promotions into open positions.
	•	Lateral transfers between departments.
	•	Transfers between teams within the same department.
	•	Transfers to positions in another business unit.
	•	Internal applications for positions in another geographic location, subject to applicable company and immigration requirements.
This policy does not apply to temporary project assignments, short-term rotations, internships, or contractor engagements.

3. Definitions
Internal Job Switch
An internal job switch is a permanent transfer of an employee from their current position to another approved position within Acme Technologies.
Current Manager
The manager responsible for the employee's current position.
Hiring Manager
The manager responsible for the open position to which the employee is applying.
Internal Candidate
An existing eligible employee who applies for an internally posted position.
Notice / Transition Period
The period required for an employee to transition responsibilities from their current role to their new role.
Critical Business Period
A period during which an employee's immediate transfer may create significant operational risk. Examples may include a major product launch, regulatory deadline, or critical customer implementation.

4. Policy Statement
Acme Technologies supports internal career mobility.
Eligible employees may apply for internally posted positions through the company's internal career portal. Employees are not required to resign from their current position before applying for another internal position.
Selection decisions are based on job-related factors such as:
	•	Skills and qualifications.
	•	Relevant experience.
	•	Job requirements.
	•	Interview performance.
	•	Demonstrated performance and behaviors.
	•	Business requirements...
Applying for an internal position does not guarantee selection or transfer.

5. Eligibility Requirements
An employee may apply for an internal position when all applicable eligibility requirements are satisfied.
Standard eligibility
Employees must generally:
	1	Be a permanent employee of the company.
	2	Have completed at least 12 months in their current role.
	3	Have a satisfactory performance record.
	4	Not be serving a formal notice period.
	5	Not be subject to an active disciplinary suspension.
	6	Meet the qualifications specified in the job description.
Exceptions
Exceptions to the 12-month requirement may be considered when:
	•	The employee is applying for a company-designated critical skill position.
	•	The employee's current position is being eliminated or substantially changed.
	•	The employee is participating in an approved organizational restructuring.
	•	HR and the relevant business leaders approve an exception.
Employees should contact HR if they believe an exception applies.

6. Internal Job Posting
All positions eligible for internal applications should be posted on the company's internal career portal.
A typical internal job posting includes:
	•	Job title.
	•	Department.
	•	Location.
	•	Job level.
	•	Hiring manager.
	•	Required qualifications.
	•	Preferred qualifications.
	•	Application deadline.
	•	Expected work arrangement.
	•	Salary grade or compensation information, where applicable.
Internal employees should use the company's internal career portal to submit applications.

7. Application Process
Employees wishing to change jobs internally should follow these steps.
Step 1: Review the Position
The employee reviews the internal job posting and confirms that they meet the stated requirements.
Step 2: Submit an Application
The employee submits their application through the internal career portal.
The application may include:
	•	Current resume.
	•	Relevant skills.
	•	Certifications.
	•	Previous experience.
	•	Career profile.
	•	Responses to screening questions.
Step 3: Initial Screening
The hiring team reviews eligible internal applications against the requirements of the position.
Step 4: Interview
Shortlisted candidates may participate in one or more interviews.
Depending on the position, the process may include:
	•	Hiring manager interview.
	•	Technical interview.
	•	Panel interview.
	•	Leadership interview.
	•	Skills assessment.
Step 5: Selection
The hiring manager selects the candidate based on the requirements of the position and the company's hiring process.
Step 6: Transfer Discussion
If an employee is selected, HR coordinates with the current manager and hiring manager to establish a mutually agreed transition date.
Step 7: Role Transition
The employee completes required knowledge transfer and other transition activities before joining the new position.

8. Manager Notification
Employees may apply for an internal position without obtaining advance approval from their current manager.
If an employee becomes a finalist or is selected, HR will coordinate the appropriate manager notification process.
Managers should not discourage employees from exploring legitimate internal career opportunities.
Managers must not retaliate against an employee for applying for an internal position.
Examples of prohibited retaliation include:
	•	Threatening an employee because they applied.
	•	Reducing opportunities solely because they applied.
	•	Giving an unjustified negative performance assessment because they applied.
	•	Blocking an approved transfer without a legitimate business reason.

9. Confidentiality
Internal applications should be handled confidentially.
Information about an employee's application should be shared only with individuals who have a legitimate business need to know.
Interviewers and hiring managers should not unnecessarily disclose an employee's application status.
Employees should also exercise discretion when discussing internal applications.

10. Performance Considerations
An employee's performance may be considered during the internal hiring process.
Performance considerations may include:
	•	Most recent performance review.
	•	Achievement of role expectations.
	•	Demonstrated competencies.
	•	Relevant project contributions.
	•	Compliance with company policies.
A manager should not provide misleading or intentionally inaccurate information about an employee.
Performance information must be factual, relevant, and consistent with company performance-management processes.

11. Compensation Changes
An internal job switch may or may not result in a compensation adjustment.
Compensation is determined according to:
	•	New role level.
	•	Salary range.
	•	Internal equity.
	•	Employee's current compensation.
	•	Location.
	•	Applicable compensation guidelines.
	•	Promotion or transfer rules.
Any compensation change will be communicated to the employee before the transfer is finalized.

12. Transition Period
The standard internal transfer transition period is 30 calendar days after the transfer has been formally approved.
The transition period may be adjusted based on business requirements.
Possible transition activities include:
	•	Knowledge transfer.
	•	Documentation.
	•	Customer handover.
	•	Project handover.
	•	Access transition.
	•	Introduction to the new team.
	•	Completion of critical deliverables.
The current manager and hiring manager should work together to avoid unnecessary delays.
In exceptional circumstances, HR may approve a transition period longer or shorter than 30 days.

13. Relocation and Location Changes
If the new position is based in another city or country, additional requirements may apply.
These may include:
	•	Relocation approval.
	•	Immigration or work authorization.
	•	Tax requirements.
	•	Employment-law requirements.
	•	Relocation benefits.
	•	Changes to employment terms.
An internal job offer does not automatically guarantee relocation assistance.

14. Reapplication
An employee who is not selected for an internal position may apply for other internal positions, subject to the eligibility requirements of those positions.
There is no mandatory waiting period after an unsuccessful internal application unless otherwise specified for a particular program.
Employees are encouraged to request feedback from the hiring team where available.

15. Withdrawal of Application
Employees may withdraw an internal application at any point before accepting a formal internal offer.
If an employee accepts an internal offer and subsequently wishes to withdraw, they should contact HR immediately.
The company may review the circumstances and determine whether the original position remains available.

16. Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest related to an internal recruitment process.
For example, an employee should disclose if:
	•	A close relative is the hiring manager.
	•	The employee has a personal relationship with an interviewer that could affect the process.
	•	The employee has access to confidential selection information.
HR may modify the interview or selection process when appropriate.

17. Equal Opportunity
Acme Technologies provides equal employment opportunities.
Internal selection decisions must not be based on protected characteristics or other prohibited grounds under applicable law.
The company expects hiring teams to apply consistent and job-related selection criteria.

18. Policy Violations
Violations of this policy may be reviewed by HR.
Examples include:
	•	Retaliating against an employee for applying internally.
	•	Providing intentionally false information during an internal hiring process.
	•	Manipulating an internal selection process.
	•	Sharing confidential candidate information without authorization.
	•	Attempting to block an employee's transfer without legitimate business justification.
Depending on the circumstances, violations may result in corrective or disciplinary action.

19. Roles and Responsibilities
Employee
The employee is responsible for:
	•	Reviewing eligibility requirements.
	•	Providing accurate application information.
	•	Participating professionally in interviews.
	•	Completing required transition activities.
	•	Protecting confidential information.
Current Manager
The current manager is responsible for:
	•	Supporting a professional transition.
	•	Providing accurate performance information when requested.
	•	Coordinating knowledge transfer.
	•	Avoiding retaliation or unreasonable obstruction.
Hiring Manager
The hiring manager is responsible for:
	•	Following the internal recruitment process.
	•	Evaluating candidates using job-related criteria.
	•	Maintaining candidate confidentiality.
	•	Coordinating with HR and the current manager.
Human Resources
HR is responsible for:
	•	Maintaining this policy.
	•	Advising employees and managers.
	•	Supporting internal recruitment.
	•	Handling exceptions.
	•	Resolving policy-related concerns.

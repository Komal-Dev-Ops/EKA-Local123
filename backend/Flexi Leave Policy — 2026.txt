Flexi Leave Policy — 2026
Document Type: Company Policy Policy Name: Flexi Leave Policy Policy Year: 2026 Policy Version: 1.0 Effective Date: January 1, 2026 Review Date: December 31, 2026 Policy Owner: Human Resources Applies To: All eligible full-time and part-time employees Status: Active

1. Purpose
The purpose of this Flexi Leave Policy is to provide employees with flexibility to take paid time off for personal, cultural, religious, family, or other individual needs.
The policy allows employees to select certain leave days from a company-approved list of optional holidays rather than requiring every employee to observe the same optional holidays.
This policy is intended to:
	•	Support employee flexibility and work-life balance.
	•	Accommodate different cultural and religious practices.
	•	Provide employees with greater choice in planning personal time off.
	•	Establish consistent rules for requesting, approving, and recording flexi leave.

2. Scope
This policy applies to eligible employees of the company during the 2026 calendar year.
The policy applies to employees working from company offices, approved remote locations, and hybrid work arrangements.
Contractors, consultants, interns, temporary workers, and other non-employee personnel are not eligible for flexi leave unless their employment or engagement agreement specifically provides otherwise.

3. Definition of Flexi Leave
Flexi Leave is paid leave that an eligible employee may use for selected optional holidays or personal occasions from the company's approved Flexi Leave Calendar.
Flexi leave is different from mandatory company holidays. Mandatory holidays are designated by the company and apply to eligible employees according to the company's holiday calendar.
Flexi leave is employee-selected and subject to the eligibility, notice, approval, and usage requirements described in this policy.

4. Flexi Leave Entitlement for 2026
Each eligible employee receives 3 Flexi Leave days during the 2026 calendar year.
The annual entitlement is:
	•	Flexi Leave Entitlement: 3 days per calendar year.
	•	Leave Type: Paid leave.
	•	Allocation Period: January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026.
	•	Maximum Flexi Leave Usage: 3 days during the 2026 calendar year.
	•	Unused Flexi Leave: Does not automatically carry forward to 2027.
	•	Cash Conversion: Flexi Leave cannot be converted into cash unless required by applicable law or expressly approved under another company policy.
Employees who join the company during 2026 may receive a prorated Flexi Leave entitlement based on their date of joining.

5. Eligibility
An employee is eligible for Flexi Leave if the employee:
	1	Is an active employee of the company.
	2	Has completed any applicable minimum service requirement under their employment terms.
	3	Is not serving a notice period, unless HR approves otherwise.
	4	Has sufficient unused Flexi Leave entitlement available at the time of the request.
Eligibility may be subject to applicable employment laws and the employee's employment agreement.

7. Selecting Flexi Leave
Employees may select any eligible date from the approved Flexi Leave Calendar, subject to manager approval and business requirements.
An employee does not need to provide a religious or personal justification for selecting an approved Flexi Leave date.
Employees may use multiple Flexi Leave days consecutively if:
	•	The requested dates are eligible Flexi Leave dates.
	•	The employee has sufficient entitlement.
	•	The request is approved by the employee's manager.

8. Leave Request Process
Employees should submit a Flexi Leave request through the company's designated HR or leave-management system.
The request should normally be submitted at least 5 working days before the requested leave date.
For planned leave around high-demand periods, employees may be asked to provide additional notice.
A leave request should include:
	•	Employee name or employee identifier.
	•	Requested Flexi Leave date.
	•	Number of leave days requested.
	•	Manager or approver.
	•	Any required work handover information.

9. Approval Process
Flexi Leave is subject to approval based on business requirements and team availability.
Managers should consider:
	•	Existing approved leave within the team.
	•	Business-critical activities.
	•	Customer or project commitments.
	•	Required staffing levels.
	•	Employee workload and handover arrangements.
Managers should apply the approval process consistently and avoid discrimination based on an employee's religion, culture, personal beliefs, gender, or other protected characteristic.
Approval should normally be provided or declined through the company's designated leave-management system.

10. Leave During Probation
Employees on probation may use Flexi Leave if their employment terms and applicable company rules permit it.
If Flexi Leave is restricted during probation, the employee may become eligible after completing the applicable probation or minimum-service period.
The employee's employment agreement and applicable law take precedence where they provide greater rights.

11. Flexi Leave During Notice Period
Employees serving a notice period may request Flexi Leave.
Approval is subject to:
	•	Available leave balance.
	•	Business requirements.
	•	Required transition and handover activities.
	•	Applicable employment terms.
The company may restrict planned leave during the notice period where permitted by applicable law and employment terms.

12. Unused Flexi Leave
Unused Flexi Leave expires at the end of the applicable calendar year unless the company explicitly announces a carry-forward exception.
For the 2026 policy year, unused Flexi Leave will normally expire on December 31, 2026.
Unused Flexi Leave will not automatically be transferred to the 2027 leave balance.

13. Cancellation or Change of Leave
An employee may cancel or change an approved Flexi Leave request through the designated leave-management system.
Cancellation or modification is subject to manager approval where required.
If an employee cancels a Flexi Leave request before the leave date, the corresponding leave balance will normally be restored.

14. Unplanned Absence
Flexi Leave should not normally be used as a substitute for emergency, sick, or other statutory leave.
If an employee is unable to attend work because of an unexpected event, the employee should follow the applicable emergency, sick leave, or absence-reporting procedure.

15. Half-Day Flexi Leave
The company may permit Flexi Leave to be taken as a half day where the leave-management system supports half-day leave.
A half-day Flexi Leave request will consume 0.5 day from the employee's available Flexi Leave balance.
The applicable working-hours rules of the employee's location will determine the definition of a half day.

16. Interaction With Other Leave Types
Flexi Leave is separate from other leave categories such as:
	•	Annual or earned leave.
	•	Sick leave.
	•	Casual leave.
	•	Maternity or parental leave.
	•	Bereavement leave.
	•	Compensatory leave.
	•	Statutory leave.
The employee may combine Flexi Leave with another leave category where permitted by company policy and applicable law.

17. Public Holidays and Weekly Days Off
If a selected Flexi Leave date falls on a company-declared mandatory holiday, weekly day off, or another non-working day for the employee, the employee will not normally receive an additional Flexi Leave day.
Employees should verify the applicable holiday calendar before submitting a Flexi Leave request.

18. Remote and Hybrid Employees
Employees working remotely or under a hybrid arrangement are subject to the same Flexi Leave entitlement and approval rules as office-based employees, unless a location-specific policy provides otherwise.
Employees should follow the holiday calendar applicable to their employment location.

19. Location-Specific Holidays
Where employees are governed by different regional holiday calendars, the company may publish location-specific Flexi Leave calendars.
Employees should select Flexi Leave dates from the calendar applicable to their official employment location.
A transfer to another location may result in changes to the employee's applicable holiday calendar.

20. Manager Responsibilities
Managers are responsible for:
	•	Reviewing Flexi Leave requests promptly.
	•	Applying approval criteria consistently.
	•	Maintaining appropriate team coverage.
	•	Avoiding discriminatory or inconsistent treatment.
	•	Ensuring employees are not discouraged from using eligible Flexi Leave without a legitimate business reason.
	•	Escalating policy questions to HR when necessary.

21. Employee Responsibilities
Employees are responsible for:
	•	Reviewing their available Flexi Leave balance.
	•	Submitting requests within the required notice period.
	•	Obtaining approval before taking planned Flexi Leave.
	•	Maintaining appropriate work handovers.
	•	Recording leave accurately in the designated system.
	•	Following applicable absence-reporting procedures.

22. HR Responsibilities
Human Resources is responsible for:
	•	Maintaining the Flexi Leave Policy.
	•	Publishing the annual Flexi Leave Calendar.
	•	Maintaining leave-management records.
	•	Providing guidance on policy interpretation.
	•	Monitoring policy compliance.
	•	Communicating material policy changes to employees.

23. Policy Exceptions
Exceptions to this policy require approval from HR and, where applicable, the relevant business or functional leader.
Exceptions should be documented and applied consistently.
Nothing in this policy is intended to reduce rights provided to employees under applicable law, employment agreements, collective agreements, or other binding requirements.

24. Compliance With Applicable Law
This policy will be administered in accordance with applicable employment and labor laws.
If a provision of this policy conflicts with a mandatory legal requirement, the applicable legal requirement will prevail.
Employees may contact HR for clarification regarding location-specific statutory leave requirements.

25. Policy Changes
The company may amend, replace, or withdraw this policy when necessary.
Material changes will be communicated through the company's normal employee communication channels.
The latest approved version published by Human Resources will be considered the controlling version of this policy.

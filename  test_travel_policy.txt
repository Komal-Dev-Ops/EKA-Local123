COMPANY TRAVEL POLICY

Document ID: HR-TRAVEL-001
Department: Human Resources
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Review Date: December 31, 2026

1. PURPOSE

This travel policy defines the rules and guidelines for employees traveling for official business purposes. It covers travel approval, transportation, accommodation, meals, travel expenses, and reimbursement procedures.

2. TRAVEL ELIGIBILITY

All full-time employees are eligible for reimbursement of reasonable and necessary business travel expenses when the travel is approved by their reporting manager.

Contract employees and consultants may claim travel expenses only when their contract or engagement agreement specifically permits such reimbursement.

3. TRAVEL APPROVAL

Employees must obtain approval from their reporting manager before booking business travel.

Travel requests should normally be submitted at least 5 business days before the planned travel date.

For international travel, approval from the department head is also required.

Employees should not book non-refundable tickets before receiving the required approvals.

4. AIR TRAVEL

Domestic air travel is permitted for business trips where the total travel distance exceeds 500 kilometers or where air travel is considered more practical by the reporting manager.

Economy class must be used for domestic flights.

Employees should select reasonably priced flights that meet business requirements.

Premium economy and business class travel require written approval from the department head.

5. TRAIN TRAVEL

Employees may use train travel for domestic business trips.

AC Chair Car, AC 2 Tier, and AC 3 Tier are reimbursable depending on the duration and business requirements of the journey.

First AC travel requires prior approval from the department head.

6. LOCAL TRANSPORTATION

Employees may claim reasonable expenses for taxis, app-based transportation, public transportation, and airport transfers used for official business.

Employees should use economical transportation whenever practical.

Personal transportation expenses unrelated to business activities are not reimbursable.

7. HOTEL ACCOMMODATION

Employees may stay in hotels that meet company-approved standards.

The maximum hotel reimbursement limit for domestic travel is INR 5,000 per night, including applicable taxes.

For metropolitan cities, the maximum limit is INR 7,000 per night.

Any accommodation exceeding the applicable limit requires prior written approval from the department head.

8. MEAL EXPENSES

Employees can claim reasonable meal expenses during approved business travel.

The maximum daily meal allowance for domestic travel is INR 1,200 per day.

Breakfast, lunch, and dinner are covered within the daily limit.

Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable.

Personal entertainment expenses are not reimbursable.

9. TRAVEL ADVANCE

Employees may request a travel advance before starting an approved business trip.

The travel advance request should be submitted at least 3 business days before departure.

Employees must submit all supporting bills and expense details after completing the trip.

Any unused travel advance must be returned to the company.

10. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT

Employees must submit travel expense claims within 10 business days after returning from the business trip.

The claim must include:

- Travel tickets
- Hotel invoices
- Meal receipts, where applicable
- Local transportation receipts
- Other supporting documents

Expenses without appropriate supporting documents may be rejected.

11. CANCELLATION CHARGES

Cancellation charges for business travel are reimbursable when the cancellation is caused by a business requirement or an unavoidable situation.

Personal cancellation charges are normally not reimbursable.

12. INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL

International business travel requires approval from the reporting manager and department head.

Employees must ensure that they have valid passports, visas, and other required travel documents.

Business class international travel requires specific written approval from the department head.

13. EXPENSE SUBMISSION PROCESS

Employees should submit their expenses through the company's expense management system.

The employee must select the appropriate expense category and upload supporting documents.

The finance team will review the submitted expenses.

Approved reimbursements are normally processed within 7 business days after approval.

14. POLICY VIOLATIONS

Expenses that violate this policy may be rejected.

Repeated violations may be reported to the employee's reporting manager and Human Resources department.

Employees should contact the Finance or Human Resources department if they are unsure whether an expense is reimbursable.

15. CONTACT INFORMATION

Finance Department
